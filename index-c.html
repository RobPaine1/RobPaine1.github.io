Things I Have Done

Community building for NEST (Network for EA Support and Training):

  • I'm helping universities in the DC area run their Effective Altruism groups, primarily Georgetown.

Contracting for 80,000 Hours, a nonprofit advising people who want to have high impact careers (currently focusing on risks from AI):

  • I found new jobs and opportunities for the 80,000 Hours Job Board on a weekly basis while maintaining sourcing automation pipelines. I also suggested and got through some product improvements.
  • I filtered applicants for 80,000 Hours Advising in collaboration with the advising department.

Co-organizing Effective Altruism at UT Austin:

  • I organized weekly meetups and socials, including faculty guest speakers. I took care of a lot of marketing tasks like tabling, posters and social media. I also ran an Introductory Effective Altruism fellowship and contributed to our annual retreats.
